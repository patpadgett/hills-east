How a session comes together.

  1. Talk
  2. Track
  3. Finish

Talk it through

Send John the songs, rough demos, or a voice memo, plus a couple of records you want yours to sit next to. He’ll come back with a plan, a rough timeline, and straight answers.

Get in the room

Load in, get comfortable, dial the sounds at the source. The session runs at the pace of the performance — nobody watches the clock louder than the music.

Finish and release

Mixes go back and forth until they feel right. Then finals, formats, and a clear list of what you’re walking away with — ready for release.
