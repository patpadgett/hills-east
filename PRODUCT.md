# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (confirmed by Patrick's standing convention: flat HTML/CSS, custom assets, README). Deploy target: GitHub Pages (the prior build deployed via Actions to patpadgett/john-matthews-recording-studio).

## Users

Independent musicians, bands, and vocalists in the Kansas City metro looking for a room to record in and someone to finish the record. Situation: they have songs, maybe references, and want to know what this room is, whether it fits them, and how to get in touch with John.

Secondary: Patrick (site owner/builder) who will publish and later fill in John's contact details.

## Product Purpose

A single-page website for Hills East Recording, John Matthews' recording studio in Lenexa, Kansas. Success: a visitor understands it is a real studio run by a real person, feels the room's character, and sends John an inquiry through the site's form.

## Positioning

Open decision. Patrick does not yet know John's genres, room type (home vs commercial), whether he mixes/masters, gear, or credits. Nothing about these may be claimed; copy stays honest and general until John supplies facts.

## Operating Context

Recording sessions, mixing, finishing records; conversation-first booking (talk it through, then get in the room). Lenexa, KS — Johnson County, Kansas City metro.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact form on the page (POST-ready, dummy success state) is the only booking path until phone/email arrive.
- Phone, email, Instagram, booking link: PLACEHOLDER — Patrick will supply later. Never invent them.
- No equipment list, rates, credits, testimonials, client names. Do not fabricate.
- Studio location may be stated as Lenexa, Kansas (confirmed).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Hills East Recording (confirmed). Owner: John Matthews.
- The looping studio video `Hills_East_Recording.mp4` (560×560, 16 fps, 6.06 s) must anchor the top of the site (Patrick's explicit request). Poster: `assets/hills-east-poster.jpg`.
- Patrick's conventions: action-oriented, non-self-referential CTAs; no internal/meta language in copy; distinctive, not template-like.

## Evidence on Hand

- `Hills_East_Recording.mp4` — animated studio interior loop.
- `assets/hills-east-poster.jpg` — first-frame poster.
- Nothing else. No photos, logo, audio samples, or credits exist yet.

## Product Principles

1. Say only what is true; leave visible, labeled slots for what John will supply.
2. The room is the pitch — let the video and its atmosphere carry the first viewport.
3. One action: reach John. Every section should return the visitor to the form.
4. Feel like a specific place in Lenexa, not a stock "studio" template.
