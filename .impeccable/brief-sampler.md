# Hidden rack sampler · Operate

Local extension of the established rack. Preserve site, booking patchbay, drum machine and monosynth. User pins MPC-inspired 4×4 hardware; no manufacturer affiliation. Code-led, no image assets required.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One playable pad bank becomes the selected sound's step pattern, not a second unrelated grid.
OWN-WORLD: Cream chassis, graphite rubber pads, red silkscreen, green-gray LCD; local equipment typography.
STORY: Trigger a sound, switch mode, program its rhythm, run all sixteen independent patterns on the rack clock.
FIRST VIEWPORT: LCD and physical controls left, large 4×4 pad bed right; stacked on phones. Red transport below display. Signature interaction: pad labels become step states in place.
FORM: Precisely specified local extension; no seed required. Motion is short pad depression and clock chase, with reduced-motion support.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
